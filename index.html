About

Crafting emotion, one frame at a time

Stories rooted in personal and cultural identity, shot with raw feeling and honest light.

Senula Herath is a Toronto filmmaker and cinematographer. A graduate of George Brown Polytechnic's Video, Design & Production program, he writes, directs, shoots, lights and cuts his own films, and crews on others' as cinematographer, gaffer, key grip, assistant director, script supervisor and sound recordist.

He is developing his next short with a close creative team under his banner, D31 Films.
